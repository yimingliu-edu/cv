Professional summary

Doctoral researcher in Educational Technology and Assessment at The University of Hong Kong. My research spans learning analytics, computational psychometrics, game-based assessment and learning, self-regulated learning, collaborative problem solving, and AI in education.

I work on the Learning and Assessment for Digital Citizenship project (PI: Prof. Nancy Law), co-developing Digital City Fighter, a 3D multiplayer mobile role-playing game for assessing primary school students' collaborative problem solving skills, and CPSLens, a learning analytics dashboard for teachers. My dissertation examines game-based assessment of collaborative problem solving skills for primary school students (supervisors: Prof. Nancy Law & Prof. Xiao Hu). I completed an M.S. in Educational Technology at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (advisor: Prof. Jerry Chih-Yuan Sun) and dual B.A. degrees in Chinese Language and Literature and Finance (double major) at Jinan University.

Research Experience (Selected)

Worked on the TRS project titled Learning and Assessment for Digital Citizenship (PI: Prof. Nancy Law) at the Faculty of Education, University of Hong Kong

Co-developed Digital City Fighter, a 3D multiplayer mobile role-playing game designed to assess primary school students' collaborative problem solving skills.

Co-developed CPSLens, a learning analytics dashboard enabling teachers to visualize and evaluate students' in-game collaborative problem solving performance.

Wrote the research proposal (supervised by Prof. Nancy Law and Prof. Xiao Hu), which included:

  • Conducting extensive literature reviews and formulating research questions.
  • Establishing theoretical frameworks and devising a multimodal coding scheme.
  • Adopting evidence-centered design and developing the experimental protocol.

Conducted in-school experiments, collecting multimodal data from 100 teams (N = 400 students), including log files, verbal communications, and survey responses.

Served as the primary coder, coding over 115,000 lines of multimodal data based on the coding scheme, achieving high inter-rater reliability.

Analyzed the coded data via quantitative analyses (e.g., psychometric network analysis, multilevel SEM, and data visualization) and drafted journal manuscripts.

Worked on the project titled The Application of Big Data in Education: From Smart Online Learning to the Flipped Classroom Integrated with Interactive Feedback Technologies (PI: Prof. Jerry Chih-Yuan Sun) at the Institute of Education, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University

  • Assisted in proposal writing for grant applications and experimental design and implementation.
  • Analyzed multimodal data using partial least squares SEM and temporal learning analytics methods to model the mechanisms of self-regulated learning.
  • Drafted manuscripts for publication and wrote up project reports.

Worked on the project titled Research of Glocally Meaningful and Relevant MOOCs Redevelopment Model: Cross-Cultural Design, Learning Analytics, and Curriculum Evaluation (PI: Prof. Ken-Zen Chen) at the Institute of Education, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University

  • Co-redesigned and localized Learning How to Learn, a renowned Coursera MOOC with over 4 million learners.
  • Developed and validated test items to measure student learning outcomes.
  • Facilitated World Café focus groups to gather qualitative student feedback.
