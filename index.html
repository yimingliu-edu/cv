Professional profile

Research focus bridging quantitative methods with classroom-ready learning analytics tools.

I work on the Learning and Assessment for Digital Citizenship project at HKU (PI: Prof. Nancy Law), co-developing the Digital City Fighter game and the CPSLens learning analytics dashboard for collaborative problem solving. My dissertation examines game-based assessment of collaborative problem solving among primary school students, supervised by Prof. Nancy Law and Prof. Xiao Hu.

Previously, I completed an M.S. in Educational Technology at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (advisor: Prof. Jerry Chih-Yuan Sun) and dual B.A. degrees in Chinese Language & Literature and Finance at Jinan University.
